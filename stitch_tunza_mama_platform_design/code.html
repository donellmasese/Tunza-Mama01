Understanding Perinatal Wellness

Breaking Stigmas Around Motherhood in Kenya

Motherhood carries immense joy alongside heavy, unspoken emotional trials. In Kenya, 1 in 5 women experience prenatal or postpartum mood and anxiety disorders. Experiencing exhaustion, fear, or sadness does not mean failure—it means you need tender care.
