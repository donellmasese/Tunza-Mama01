## Brand & Style

This design system embodies compassionate care, physical safety, and communal reassurance for Kenyan mothers navigating perinatal and postpartum mental health. The visual identity bridges clinical rigor with maternal warmth—replacing sterile healthcare tropes with an organic, nurturing aesthetic that honors Kenyan maternal journeys. 

The style synthesizes **Tactile Organic Warmth** with **Modern Soft Minimalism**:
- **Tone & Voice:** Empathetic, de-stigmatizing, reassuring, and dignified. Microcopy prioritizes affirmative phrasing ("You are held," "Rest here," "Checking in with you") over clinical pathology.
- **Visual Personality:** Soft, luminous, deeply grounded. Sun-warmed ivory tones eliminate the anxiety of stark hospital whites, while balanced pairings of royal purple, blossoming rose, and forest sage impart a restorative cadence.
- **Emotional Goals:** Reduce anxiety, evoke ancestral community care, build trust, and lower cognitive friction during high-stress transitions.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to feel protective, vibrant, and deeply grounding. All contrast ratios strictly exceed WCAG AAA standards for critical health information and AA for baseline interactive text against light backgrounds.

### Color Roles & Balance
- **Primary Canvas (`#FDFBF7` Warm Ivory Cream):** Replaces default white across all screens to reduce eye fatigue and clinical coldness.
- **Primary Purple (`#7E4A9E` to `#5C2D78`):** Represents wisdom, intuition, and mental sovereignty. Used for primary CTAs, active status triggers, and critical navigational markers.
- **Rose Pink Tier (`#E87A90` vibrant rose, `#F4A5B7` soft blush, `#FBF0F3` rose mist):** Conveys tenderness, maternal attachment, and care triage. Used for self-assessment trackers, emotional mood logs, and comforting highlights.
- **Nurturing Green Tier (`#385747` forest green, `#829B85` calming sage):** Represents holistic vitality, physical healing, and grounding nature. Used for clinical milestones, supportive provider confirmations, and successful health logs.
- **Deep Neutral (`#241A29` Aubergine Slate):** Replaces pure black for typography. Softens reading contrast while maintaining rigorous 12:1+ legibility against ivory surfaces.

### Gradient Treatments
- **Motherhood Glow (Subtle Background Canvas):** Linear gradient from `#FBF0F3` (top right) to `#FDFBF7` (bottom left) applied to empty states and top-tier headers.
- **Nurture Action (Hero Interactive):** 135deg linear gradient from `#7E4A9E` to `#5C2D78`.

## Typography

Typography relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans** for its balanced geometry, wide aperture, and organic human warmth. The letterforms are non-threatening, legible under bright sunlight on mobile displays, and inherently clear across both English and Swahili terminology.

- Headings use medium-to-bold weights with gentle negative tracking to command serene authority without intimidation.
- Body text features relaxed line heights (1.6x) to preserve effortless scanability for sleep-deprived mothers and frontline community healthcare workers.
- Labels maintain slightly boosted tracking for immediate legibility in tags, pill badges, and screening status markers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model emphasizes a warm, uncluttered rhythm that prevents visual overwhelm.

- **Mobile First Focus (Grid 4-col):** Primary operational context for Kenya's mHealth tools. Uses `margin: 1.25rem` (20px) and `gutter: 1rem` (16px). All primary thumb actions are pinned within the lower 40% vertical viewport threshold.
- **Tablet (Grid 8-col):** Accommodates community midwives and mobile clinic visits with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Desktop (Grid 12-col max 1280px):** For clinical coordinators and hospital administrators with `margin: 3.5rem`.
- **Vertical Spacing Cadence:** Built on an 8px modular baseline (`space-xs` to `space-xl`). Sections must never crowd; breathing space around maternal screening answers directly correlates with calm user completion rates.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve an organic, soothing feel, harsh directional drop-shadows are strictly prohibited. Depth is rendered via **Tonal Layering** combined with **Ambient Lavender/Rose Shadows**:

- **Level 0 (Base Surface):** Canvas layer rendered in `#FDFBF7` Warm Ivory Cream.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Modules):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface resting on ivory, surrounded by an ambient glow: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(126, 74, 158, 0.06)`. Delicately bordered by a hairline 1px stroke of `#F4A5B7` at 25% opacity.
- **Level 2 (Active Cards & Elevated Modals):** `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(92, 45, 120, 0.12)`, floating above a dimmed backdrop blur (`rgba(36, 26, 41, 0.35)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(6px)`).
- **Interactive State (Tapped / Pressed):** Pill buttons and interactive modules shift down with a tactile sink, reducing elevation to Level 0 with a gentle 1.5px inner tint.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Pill-shaped (Level 3)** geometry. Every interactive element—buttons, badge tags, search bars, and selection chips—utilizes full capsule roundness (infinite border-radius or `9999px`). 

Structural surfaces (content cards, dialogs, bottom sheets) feature generous organic corners:
- Standard modules and cards: `rounded-lg` (2rem / 32px).
- Bottom sheets, modal dialogues, and mood loggers: `rounded-xl` (3rem / 48px).
- Form inputs: Pill capsules (1.5rem / 24px minimum).

Sharp 90-degree angles are eliminated across the system to evoke safety, cradle-like embrace, and gentle support.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Full capsule (`roundedness: 3`). Solid gradient from `#7E4A9E` to `#5C2D78` with crisp `#FFFFFF` typography (`label-lg`). Minimum height 52px for mobile ergonomics. Subdued rose-tinted shadow `0px 6px 16px rgba(126, 74, 158, 0.25)`.
- **Secondary Nurture Button:** Rose Mist background (`#FBF0F3`), text `#7E4A9E`, no border.
- **Support / Crisis Action Button:** Grounded Forest Green (`#385747`) with white text and a reassuring phone or heart icon.

### Reassuring Badge Tags & Chips
- Fully rounded pills (`padding: 6px 14px`).
- **Supportive Status Tag:** `#829B85` background with deep `#1F3329` text for healthy milestones.
- **Emotional Check-in Pill:** Soft blush `#FBF0F3` base with a 1px border of `#F4A5B7`, text `#E87A90`.
- **Interactive Choice Chip (Mood / Symptoms):** Toggled state shifts from white to `#7E4A9E` background with `#FFFFFF` text and gentle spring scale.

### Cards & Modules
- Resting on `#FFFFFF` with `rounded-lg` (32px). Generous internal padding (`space-lg`).
- Top-accented screening cards use a subtle soft-curved stroke at the top boundary in `#E87A90`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Pill-shaped field borders (28px radius). Background `#FFFFFF`.
- Inactive border: 1.5px solid `#EFEBE4`.
- Focus border: 2px solid `#7E4A9E` accompanied by an ambient purple halo glow (`rgba(126, 74, 158, 0.15)`).
- Floating empathetic helper text underneath in deep neutral muted (`#615266`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Radios use concentric circular rings: active state features an outer `#7E4A9E` stroke with an inner `#E87A90` core dot.
- Checkboxes feature rounded squircle corners (8px) filled with `#385747` and a white curved tickmark.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Daily Mood Blossom Tracker:** Five-point circular selection scale utilizing warm color progression (Sage -> Ivory -> Rose -> Warm Purple).
- **Mama's Affirmation Banner:** Full-width soft-curved container (`#FBF0F3`) featuring comforting typography, empathetic microcopy, and a quick-tap community midwife reassurance link.
